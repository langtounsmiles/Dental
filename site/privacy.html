Privacy, Complaints & Regulation

Last updated: November 2026

Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect and use your personal information when you register your interest in our practice, contact us, or use this website.

Who we are

Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic, Kirkcaldy, Fife.
Email: info@kirkcaldydentist.co.uk
We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

What information we collect

When you register your interest (through our Facebook or Instagram form, or the form on this website), we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Phone number and email address
  • Postcode
  • The number of people you are registering, whether they are adults or children, and whether you are currently registered with a dentist
  • The date and time you signed up, and where you heard about us (for example a leaflet)

We do not ask for medical information at this stage.

How we use your information

  • To record your interest and confirm we have your details by text and email
  • To keep you informed about our opening date and practice information
  • To answer any questions you send us

We will not use your details for unrelated marketing without your consent, and we never sell your information.

Our lawful basis

We process your information based on your consent, given when you submit the registration form, and our legitimate interest in keeping interested people informed about our opening. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to manage these sign-ups, who process data only on our instructions:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook/Instagram), which hosts the registration form
  • Hostinger, which hosts this website and its registration form
  • Google (Google Workspace / Sheets / Gmail / Apps Script), where we store sign-ups and send emails
  • Zapier, which transfers your details from the form to our records
  • ClickSend, which sends confirmation text messages

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where this happens, they use appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep this information for up to 12 months after we open, or until you ask us to remove it. If you later become a patient, your records are kept in line with professional record-keeping requirements.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. To remove your details or make a request, email info@kirkcaldydentist.co.uk.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may record your IP address.

NHS & private care

Kirkcaldy Dental & Implant Clinic is a new NHS dental practice that will also offer private treatments. Full details of NHS and private services and fees will be published before we open.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with any aspect of our service, please contact the Practice Manager at info@kirkcaldydentist.co.uk.

For NHS treatment, you can also contact the NHS Fife Patient Experience Team or the Scottish Public Services Ombudsman. For private treatment, you can contact the Dental Complaints Service.

Regulation

All dental professionals working at the practice are registered with the General Dental Council (GDC). You can check a dental professional's registration at gdc-uk.org.
